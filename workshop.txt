title=[42.22.2] Rest and Resume
description=[h1]Rest and Resume[/h1]
description=When a repeating job stops because your character is out of breath, they sit down, catch their breath and go back to the same work.
description=
description=[h2]What resumes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Exercise[/b]: the same exercise, for the minutes that were left.
description=[*][b]Chopping a tree[/b]: the same tree; the damage already done stays.
description=[*][b]Area Tasks[/b] (if installed): a job that stopped with "Not enough endurance" starts again on the same area.
description=[/list]
description=Other actions that end at the last endurance level only sit down and rest; nothing is restarted.
description=
description=[h2]Exercise that runs out of time[/h2]
description=When an exercise ends because its time is up (the fitness window allows 60 minutes at most), the same exercise starts again for the same time. If you are out of breath, you rest first. Up to 5 times in a row (option), counted apart from the resumes above. Exercise you stopped yourself (moving, Esc, Cancel, another action) is not continued. Can be turned off in the options.
description=
description=[h2]When it gives up[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]A zombie you can see comes within range (default 12 tiles), or any zombie gets within 5 tiles even unseen: you stand up.
description=[*]You move, stand up or start something else.
description=[*]Hunger, thirst, tiredness or injury goes above the set moodle level (muscle soreness after a workout does not count).
description=[*]The same work was resumed too many times in a row (default 5).
description=[*]Exercise stopped while in heavy pain (the level where vanilla won't let you start exercising).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Options[/h2]
description=Options > Mods > Rest and Resume (per player): on/off, endurance to rest to (default 95%), zombie distance, max repeats, moodle level, text above the character, rest after other actions, continue exercise when its time runs out (default on), max continues in a row (default 5).
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Client only; the server needs nothing. Made for single player and multiplayer; tested in single player on build 42.22.2, and resting after running out of breath was seen working on a dedicated server. Continuing exercise after its time runs out is not tested in multiplayer yet. Safe to add to or remove from a save. Resuming queues the same vanilla actions a player would, so the server checks them as usual.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=지구력이 떨어져 반복 작업이 멈추면 그 자리에 앉아 쉬었다가, 지구력이 돌아오면 같은 작업을 다시 시작합니다.
description=[list]
description=[*]운동: 같은 운동을 남은 시간만큼 다시 합니다.
description=[*]나무 베기: 같은 나무를 이어서 찍습니다. 이미 깎인 체력은 그대로 남아 있습니다.
description=[*]Area Tasks(설치한 경우): "지구력 부족"으로 멈춘 작업을 같은 영역에서 다시 시작합니다.
description=[/list]
description=그 밖에 지구력 마지막 단계에서 멈춘 동작은 앉아서 쉬기만 합니다.
description=운동 시간이 다 돼서 끝나면 같은 운동을 처음 고른 시간만큼 다시 시작합니다(옵션, 기본 켜짐, 최대 5번). 지쳐 있으면 먼저 쉬고 시작합니다. 직접 멈춘 운동은 이어서 하지 않습니다. 멀티에서는 아직 확인하지 않았습니다.
description=보이는 좀비가 설정 거리 안에 오거나 보이지 않는 좀비라도 5칸 안에 오거나, 직접 움직이거나, 배고픔·갈증·피곤·부상 무들이(운동 후 근육통은 제외) 설정 단계를 넘거나, 연속 재개 횟수를 넘으면 재개를 취소합니다.
description=옵션은 옵션 > 모드 > 쉬었다가 계속하기에 있습니다.
description=클라이언트 전용이라 서버에는 필요 없고, 세이브 중간에 넣고 빼도 됩니다.
description=
